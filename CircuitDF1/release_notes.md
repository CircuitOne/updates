Circuit One Software Bundle – Release Notes  
Release Date: August 29, 2026  
Bundle Version: Circuit Manager v0.7.11 · Circuit Link v0.7.18 · Circuit DF1 v0.7.4

Overview  
This update expands game compatibility and enhances motion fidelity. Highlights include support for popular Forza titles and BeamNG.drive, improved heave response, new banked surface and gear shift effects, and overall stability refinements.

What’s New    
- Added support for Forza Horizon 4, 5, 6, and Forza Motorsport 8.  
- Added BeamNG.drive support.  
- Improved heave effect – refined vertical motion for a more natural ride feel.  
- Added banked surface effect – simulates lateral tilt on cambered roads and corners.  
- Added gear shift effect – delivers tactile feedback during gear changes.  
- General stability and reliability improvements for smoother operation.
